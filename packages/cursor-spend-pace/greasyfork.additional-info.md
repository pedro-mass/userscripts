Linear-burn pace markers for the Cursor spending dashboard.

Shows where your usage **should** be on each included meter if you spread it evenly across the billing window. Helps answer: am I ahead or behind for the month (or week for Grok)?

### What you get

- **Cursor Models** and **Other Models**: monthly billing pace
- **Grok Bot**: separate weekly pace
- Vertical pace line on each bar plus short status text (`under pace`, `ahead of pace`, `on pace`)
- Light and dark theme support with accessible contrast

### Requirements

- Cursor Pro (or plan with included usage meters on the Spending page)
- Logged in at [cursor.com/dashboard/spending](https://cursor.com/dashboard/spending)
