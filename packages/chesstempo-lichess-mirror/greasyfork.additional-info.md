Mirror **ChessTempo opening training** to a paired **Lichess analysis** tab and keep the board in sync as you train.

Click **mirror in lichess** on ChessTempo; play moves on CT and the Lichess tab follows (one ply when possible, otherwise navigates to the FEN). Repertoire edits stay on ChessTempo; Lichess is read-only for engines, explorer, and LiChess Tools.

### Screenshot

![mirror in lichess button on ChessTempo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pedro-mass/userscripts/main/packages/chesstempo-lichess-mirror/docs/assets/mirror-button-0.1.22.png)

### Features

- One-click open paired Lichess analysis (`pamMirror` tab pairing)
- Live position sync from ChessTempo to Lichess
- Board orientation matches CT (including black repertoires)
